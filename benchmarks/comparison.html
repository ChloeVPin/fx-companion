fx-companion vs base fx (pin 04e0ae0)

Apple silicon, macOS 27, warm cache, ReleaseFast, medians. Lower is better everywhere.

Bottom line: state hand-off now wins at every size (44x–3,716x over the fork/exec+pipe tool-process pattern, byte-verified). Traversal improved 227→648 ms local but still loses to fx's walk (0.35x). One category won, one still open, both measured honestly.
