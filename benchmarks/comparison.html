fx-companion vs base fx (pin 04e0ae0)

Apple silicon, macOS 27, warm cache, ReleaseFast, medians. Lower is better everywhere.

Bottom line: both measured categories now win. State hand-off: 44x–3,716x over fork/exec+pipe at every size, byte-verified. Traversal: 2.05x faster than fx's own walker in-process, and end-to-end through the daemon RPC still beats fx when the daemon is spawned directly (125 ms vs 213 ms); under launchd it holds parity (~220–300 ms). All numbers from clean re-runs after fixing three real bugs (fcntl variadic ABI, fd-reuse stale-parent opens, unterminated path strings).
